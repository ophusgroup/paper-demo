## Conclusion

In conclusion, dapagliflozin significantly improves outcomes in patients with heart failure by reducing hospitalization rates and contributing to improved cardiovascular outcomes. Across multiple high-quality studies, the evidence consistently demonstrates a meaningful reduction in heart failure–related events, particularly in outpatient populations where preventing disease progression is essential ([McMurray et al., 2019](#mcmurray-2019); [Solomon et al., 2022](#solomon-2022)).

Importantly, the consistency of findings across randomized controlled trials and supporting analyses strengthens confidence in its clinical application ([Jhund et al., 2021](#jhund-2021)). As heart failure management continues to evolve, integrating therapies with proven morbidity and mortality benefits is critical. Dapagliflozin not only addresses a key gap in reducing hospitalization burden but also reflects a broader shift toward more comprehensive, outcome-driven care.

Future research should continue to explore long-term outcomes and the effectiveness of dapagliflozin in broader and diverse populations. However, based on the current body of evidence, dapagliflozin is strongly supported as an essential component of guideline-directed medical therapy in the outpatient management of heart failure.
