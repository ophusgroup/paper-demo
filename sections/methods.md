## Methods: Search Strategy and Study Selection

A comprehensive literature search was conducted using PubMed, CINAHL, and the Cochrane Library. Keywords included heart failure, dapagliflozin, SGLT2 inhibitors, hospitalization, cardiovascular mortality, outpatient, DAPA-HF, and DELIVER.

Inclusion criteria included high quality studies in adult HF populations, studies evaluating dapagliflozin, and outcomes related to hospitalization or cardiovascular mortality ([Zannad et al., 2020](#zannad-2020)). High-quality was determined as those papers reporting on trials with established methodological rigor, transparent and accurate reporting, and well-reasoned, proportionate interpretation of the data. Further, for drug efficacy on hard outcomes (e.g. CV death, HF hospitalization) inclusion parameters prioritized randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and high-quality prespecified analyses of those trials, in-line with medically standard, and audience expected, methodological approaches ([Bothwell et al., 2016](#bothwell-2016)). As such, randomized controlled trials, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and secondary analyses published between 2016 and 2026 were included. Studies focused exclusively on non-HF conditions and inpatient populations were excluded, the latter in-line with U.S. FDA guidelines, where inpatient populations are often excluded from drug efficacy reviews (clinical trials) to minimize variability, ensure safety, and maintain a high signal-to-noise ratio for demonstrating effectiveness ([U.S. Food and Drug Administration, 2018](#fda-2018), [2025](#fda-2025)). Additionally, Perplexity ([2026](#perplexity-2026)) an artificial intelligence (AI)-based large language model interface was used to assist in identifying potentially relevant randomized trials and recent systematic reviews using a prespecified set of prompts. All suggested articles were independently and manually (by this paper's author) screened against the inclusion criteria and verified in primary databases.

Seven (7) studies were selected based on relevance and methodological quality, including two landmark randomized controlled trials, prespecified secondary analyses, and multiple supporting analyses, including metanalysis. Key characteristics of included trials are summarized in [](#table-1).
